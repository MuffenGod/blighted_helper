THE BLIGHTED REALM
Blighted Dreams is a dark fantasy action-RPG modpack. Tier 1 to 5 rogue dungeons, Draugr crypts, and cataclysmic abominations known as the Seven Sins infest the world. Gather gear, conquer dungeons, master mechanics, and defeat Sin bosses.

COMBAT & ARTIFACTS
- Apotheosis: RPG affixes and socketable gems. Gem cutting tables craft gems; salvaging tables yield gem dust; reforging tables re-roll affixes.
- Combat & Curios: Fluid combo attacks and weapon skills. Curios grant passive defenses, reach, and elemental wards. The Ring of Sins is a perilous covenant relic.

THE SEVEN SINS (BOSSES)
Cataclysm bosses rule the realm:
- The Sin of Sloth (Netherite Monstrosity): Colossal trial. Resistant to blunt attacks; dodge telegraphed slams.
- The Sin of Lust (Scylla): Terror of the sunken ocean abyss.
- The Sin of Wrath (Ignis): Flaming blade master in the Nether.
Prepare before challenging any Sin: high-tier affixes, buffs, and first-aid supplies are mandatory.

SURVIVAL & ENVIRONMENT
- Temperature: Cold causes freezing; heat causes hyperthermia. Campfires, wool thermals, and coats regulate temperature. The Nether requires Cooling II; the End requires Heating III.
- First Aid: Limbs take separate damage. Bleeding needs bandages or medkits; fractures need splints; morphine numbs pain.
- Food: Nourishment maintains stamina. Rotten food causes hunger sickness.

TECHNOLOGY
- Create: Kinetic rotation, water wheels, presses, and drills form the first tier of industry.
- Advanced Tech: Modern machinery and storage unlock after kinetic foundations and conquering early bosses.
